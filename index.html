Fire security concept project

February 2026 - June 2026

This project focused on improving modern fire alarm systems by expanding their capabilities beyond traditional carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide and heat detection. Our concept combined these sensors with motion sensors and cameras capable of detecting and identifying people inside a building. We proposed a companion app or handheld system that could provide firefighters with information about where people may be trapped inside a building. This could help emergency responders save valuable time by directing them towards rooms where people are detected, rather than having to search every room individually. The project gave me experience in developing engineering concepts, identifying real world problems and designing technology around a specific safety application.

3D printer completion alert (ESP32)

June 2026 - present

This project is a 3D printer completion detector built around an ESP32. It uses a red LED while a print is in progress and a green LED when the print has completed or the printer is not printing. The system is currently fully functional, but I am continuing to develop it with plans to add features such as an LCD display for showing the printer's status and additional information.
